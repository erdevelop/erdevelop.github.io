Powder coating, PVDF or anodising?

How a façade looks after 20–30 years is largely decided by its finish. Here are the three common options compared.

Qualicoat classes

Powder coatings are classified by outdoor durability: class 1 standard polyester, class 2 super durable, class 3 highest UV resistance (e.g. fluoropolymer powder). Class 2 or 3 is preferred on prestigious and south-facing façades. A Qualicoat licence (and Seaside for coastal sites) of the coater assures quality.

PVDF

Liquid coatings with 70% PVDF resin meet high-performance specifications such as AAMA 2605; they are preferred especially for metallic colours and where gloss retention matters. Most composite panels are PVDF-coated in the factory; if profiles are coated by another method, the colour match should be approved with samples in advance.

Anodising

Not a paint but aluminium's own oxide layer; it does not peel when scratched. External façades require at least 20 µm (25 µm on coasts). Since tones can vary between batches and alloys, using the same lot and sample approval matter on large projects.

When choosing

  • The corrosivity category of the site (distance from the sea, industry).
  • Colour and gloss expectations; matching profile and panel colours.
  • Warranty period and the coater's certificates.
