Aluminium or steel sub-structure?

The load-bearing system behind composite, ceramic, stone or metal cladding directly affects the façade's service life and energy performance.

Points to watch

  • Galvanic corrosion: where aluminium touches steel or copper, use separating washers and isolation; fasteners should be stainless steel.
  • Thermal bridging: thermal break pads under the brackets improve the U value noticeably; high-performance projects prefer stainless or composite brackets.
  • Expansion: aluminium rails need a fixed and sliding bracket arrangement (one fixed, the others sliding) and gaps at profile splices.
  • Fire: on tall buildings the cavity fire barriers are as critical as the classes of cladding and insulation.
