Capped (pressure plate) or structural silicone façade?

The architect's expected look, the budget and the building height decide this choice. Here are the two systems side by side under the same headings.

When to choose capped?

When the budget is limited, the building is low to mid-rise, a fast and flexible site solution is needed, or the architecture already wants the cap lines. Mixed solutions with horizontal caps and vertical silicone (or the reverse) are also common.

When to choose structural silicone?

When a continuous, flush glass look is wanted and there is a fabricator able to manage the quality control process. The silicone bite is calculated from wind suction; tall buildings or some regulations may require an additional mechanical safety device. Application and testing of siliconed frames follow ETAG 002 / EN 15434.

Questions to ask

  • Will glass be replaced from inside or outside, and is there façade access?
  • How wide does the silicone bite come out for the wind load and pane size?
  • Does the fabricator have silicone compatibility and adhesion test records?
