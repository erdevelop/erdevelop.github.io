Worked example: from wind load to joints

Façade calculations depend on each other: wind sizes the glass, glass and wind size the mullion, the mullion sizes the bracket. Here are five calculations done in order on one building; each link opens the calculator with the same values.

Erdal Üstünol · 18.11.2026Türkçe
🌬️ Wind📏 Glass📉 Mullion🔩 Bracket🧴 Joint
  • Building: 30 m high, 40 × 20 m office in Istanbul; suburban (terrain category III).
  • Façade: capped stick curtain wall, mullion spacing 1500 mm, floor height 3600 mm, glass 1500 × 2500 mm.
  • Checked location: the worst case, corner zone A at the top floor.
  • Result: 8 toughened / 16 / 44.2 heat-strengthened glass, mullion I ≥ 550 cm⁴, bracket with 2 anchors, 18 mm joint.

1. Wind load

Basic wind velocity 28 m/s, category III, h = 30 m. The calculator gives a peak velocity pressure of qp = 1.215 kN/m² (mean velocity 27.8 m/s, turbulence 0.217). Since the element is a 3.75 m² glass pane, cpe is interpolated between cpe,10 and cpe,1.

Internal pressure (cpi +0.2 / −0.3) is included in the net load. All following steps use the corner suction of 1.80 kN/m²; it is a characteristic value, and each calculation applies its own safety factor. Open this step →

2. Glass thickness

1500 × 2500 mm insulating glass supported on four edges: 8 mm toughened outside, 44.2 laminated inside. In the first try the inner laminate was annealed float: the inner pane stress is 20.3 MPa against a design strength of 18.5 MPa — not enough. Switching the inner laminate to heat-strengthened raises the strength to 39.3 MPa and the glass passes:

Deflection governs (83%). The wind load is shared between the panes by stiffness: the 8 mm outer pane takes about 80% of it. The glass weighs 40 kg/m², 150 kg per pane, which is also needed for setting blocks and lifting equipment. Open this step →

3. Mullion deflection

The mullion hangs from each floor, span L = 3600 mm, load width 1500 mm. Line load q = 1.80 × 1.50 = 2.70 kN/m. First we try a 400 cm⁴ profile: deflection 21.1 mm against a limit of 5 + 3600/300 = 17.0 mm, and the stress of 182 MPa exceeds 145 MPa. The calculator gives the requirement: I ≥ 496 cm⁴, W ≥ 45.2 cm³.

With a deeper profile (I = 550 cm⁴, W = 52 cm³) deflection becomes 15.3 mm (90%) and stress 126 MPa (87%). If you want to keep the profile, steel reinforcement inside can cover the same need; since steel's E is three times higher, 1 cm⁴ of steel acts like about 3 cm⁴ of aluminium. The support reaction is 4.86 kN at each end and feeds the next step. Open this step →

4. Brackets and anchors

One bracket carries 1.50 × 3.60 = 5.40 m² of façade. Façade weight is taken as 60 kg/m² (glass 40 kg/m² plus profiles and other parts). The bracket is fixed to the slab edge with 2 anchors; the weight acts at 120 mm eccentricity.

The bracket itself and its welds are checked separately with the fillet weld and bolt calculators. Open this step →

5. Joint

The composite panel cladding at the slab edge has a panel length of 3600 mm, α = 24 × 10⁻⁶. Installed at 15 °C; surface temperature between −20 °C and +60 °C (light colour). The panel closes the joint by 3.89 mm when heated and opens it by 3.02 mm when cooled. For a sealant with ±25% movement capability:

3.89 ÷ 0.25 = 15.6 mm + 2 mm installation tolerance → 18 mm joint

Sealant depth 9 mm (width/depth 2:1), with a Ø25 mm closed-cell backer rod behind. For a dark panel use 80 °C as the upper temperature; the joint grows. Open this step →
