What will you learn?

The questions are about amoebas, paramecia, euglena, Stentor, Didinium, diatoms, Volvox, Noctiluca, copepods, water fleas, rotifers, tardigrades, Vorticella, foraminifera, coccolithophores, hydra and viruses. After each question you get a short explanation and a link to the organism's encyclopedia page. To see the organisms in action, try the lab or the game Organizma.
