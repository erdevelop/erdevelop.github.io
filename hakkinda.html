Ben kimim

Merhaba, ben Erdal Üstünol. Cephe ve imalat sektöründe serbest (freelance) 3D CAD tasarımcısı olarak çalışıyorum; öncesinde METAL YAPI’da 3D Senior Designer’dım. Günlük işim parametrik tasarım, cephe panelleme ve 3B modelleme; Rhino, Grasshopper, SolidWorks, CATIA ve AutoCAD ile çalışıyorum.

Nasıl çalışıyor

  • Ücretsiz: Araçların hepsi ücretsiz. Sitenin giderleri reklamlarla (Google AdSense) karşılanır.
  • Dosyaların cihazında kalır: PDF, resim ve dosya araçları işi tarayıcında yapar; dosyaların bir sunucuya yüklenmez.
  • Hesap şart değil: Eğitim ilerlemeni farklı cihazlarda görmek istersen isteğe bağlı bir hesap açabilirsin; hesapsız da her şey kullanılabilir.

About

erdevelop is Erdal Üstünol's personal website: free tools, calculators, tutorials, language clubs and games.

Who I am

Hi, I'm Erdal Üstünol. I work as a freelance 3D CAD designer in the façade and manufacturing industry; before that I was a 3D Senior Designer at METAL YAPI. My daily work is parametric design, façade panelization and 3D modelling with Rhino, Grasshopper, SolidWorks, CATIA and AutoCAD.

I've been into software and computers since I was a kid. I started my career in mechanical technical drawing and later studied computer programming. Over time, the small programs and tools I wrote to make my own work easier kept growing, so I built this site to put them in one place where anyone can use them.

How it works

  • Free: All tools are free. The site's costs are covered by ads (Google AdSense).
  • Your files stay on your device: PDF, image and file tools do the work in your browser; your files are not uploaded to a server.
  • No account needed: You can optionally create an account to sync tutorial progress across devices; everything works without one.
  • For information only: Calculator results are estimates and are not engineering, financial or legal advice. See the Terms of Use (in Turkish).
